# CPI Inflation Analysis — Excel Case Study

Case study from the **Introduction to Analytics & Excel** module (Data Analytics program, Coding Ninjas): analyzing India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation across sectors, categories and regions.

## Dataset

`cpi_inflation_data.csv` — 372 rows × 30 columns

- **Sectors:** Rural, Urban, Rural+Urban (combined)
- **Period:** monthly, January 2013 – 2023
- **Categories:** cereals, meat & fish, egg, milk & products, oils & fats, fruits, vegetables, pulses, sugar, spices, non-alcoholic beverages, prepared meals/snacks, food & beverages, pan/tobacco/intoxicants, clothing, footwear, housing, fuel & light, household goods & services, health, transport & communication, recreation, education, personal care, miscellaneous, general index
- Values are CPI index numbers (base 2012 = 100)

## Case study objectives

- Monthly and annual CPI trend analysis
- Sectoral variation: food & beverages, pan/tobacco/intoxicants, fuel & light, housing, clothing & footwear
- Regional variation: urban vs rural
- Year-on-year inflation computation
- Visualizations: heat maps, slicers, pivot charts
- Excel dashboard + summary presentation with policy implications

## Tools

Microsoft Excel — pivot tables, pivot charts, slicers, conditional formatting, YoY formulas.

## Analysis workbook

`CPI_Inflation_Case_Study.xlsb` — the completed Excel workbook: raw data, missing-value handling, and question-wise analysis sheets (monthly/annual trends, YoY inflation, regional comparisons).
